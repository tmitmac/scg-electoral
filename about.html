About the Electoral Commission

The Electoral Commission of Square Country is responsible for the administration of parliamentary elections, referendums, electoral registration, and the regulation of political finance under electoral law.

Independent

The Commission operates independently from Ministers and political parties.

Electoral Law

We enforce and administer electoral legislation and party funding rules.

Our Role

Statutory responsibilities

The Electoral Commission was established under the Electoral Act 1986 as the national authority responsible for administering democratic processes within Square Country.

The Commission delivers parliamentary elections and referendums, maintains the national electoral roll, registers political parties, monitors electoral finance requirements, and provides public information regarding voting and participation in the democratic process.

The Commission also works with local electoral officials, government agencies, accessibility organisations, and community groups to ensure elections are secure, transparent, and accessible to all eligible voters.

The Commission

The Electoral Commission is governed by Commissioners appointed in accordance with the Electoral Act.

Commissioners are responsible for oversight of the organisation, ensuring electoral processes remain independent, lawful, and trusted by the public.

Operational responsibilities are carried out by the Chief Electoral Officer and Commission staff located throughout Square Country.

National Operations

The Commission coordinates nationwide election operations, including polling places, advance voting services, election staffing, counting procedures, and official result declarations.

During general elections, temporary election offices and voting locations are established across the country to support voters domestically and overseas.

Public education campaigns are conducted regularly to encourage electoral participation and awareness of voting rights.
