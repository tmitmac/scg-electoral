New Voting Centres Announced

The Square Country Electoral Commission has confirmed additional voting centres to improve access and reduce wait times for the upcoming election.

These centres will be operational on Advance Voting Days and Election Day. Voters may cast their ballot at any voting centre nationwide, regardless of electorate. However, delays should be expected for out-of-electorate voting.

  • • All listed centres are wheelchair accessible
  • • Assistance is available for voters with disabilities
  • • Voters may bring a support person
  • • Language assistance is available at select locations
